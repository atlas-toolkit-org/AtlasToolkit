# Code of Conduct

Atlas Toolkit follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:
- Be kind and patient with everyone, whatever their experience, background or identity.
- Discuss the work, not the person.
- Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not accepted in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other
  project space.

Report unacceptable behaviour to **hello@atlas-toolkit.org**. Reports are read by the maintainer and kept
confidential. The maintainer may remove content, or block people who break this code, as the Covenant's enforcement
guidelines describe.
