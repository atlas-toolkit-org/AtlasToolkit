All selected PBR height/displacement maps are released under CC0-1.0. They may be copied, modified and
redistributed, including commercially. Source credit is retained as a courtesy. Atlas does not claim authorship
of the source maps. Prepared height tiles and locally generated relief thumbnails are derived from those maps.
No source website logos, product renders or Substance generator files are bundled.

Source URLs, map names, source precision, original archive/map hashes, prepared file hashes and exact processing
settings are recorded in atlas_toolkit/tools/mesh_texture/assets/catalog.json. The reusable preparation script
is scripts/prepare_surface_textures.py. Complete source archives are not needed at runtime.
